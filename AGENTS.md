# AGENTS.md

Instructions for AI coding agents working in GODMIX are in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md). They apply to every agent, not only Claude. Read it before doing anything.

Most important: GODMIX ships with neutral mastering defaults. Before mastering or tuning anything for someone's album, ask them about their music (genre, references, where it's heard, loud or open, the album's quiet songs) and save their answers in `local/render_cfg.json`. The questions and a table of starting points are in CLAUDE.md, under "First time with a new user".
